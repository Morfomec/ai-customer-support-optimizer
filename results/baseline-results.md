This file contains the original AI responses generated using the baseline prompt.

These results will be evaluated and compared with the results from the optimized prompt later.

## TC01 — Duplicate Payment

### Input

I was charged ₹500 twice for my subscription. Please refund the extra payment.

### Baseline AI Response

Hi, I’m sorry for the duplicate charge. I understand that you were charged ₹500 twice for your subscription.

We’ll review the payment and, if the duplicate charge is confirmed, refund the extra ₹500. Please share your payment/transaction details so we can check this for you.

## TC03 — Forgot Password

### Input

I forgot my password and cannot log into my account.

### Baseline AI Response

Hi, I’m sorry you’re having trouble logging in. You can reset your password by selecting **“Forgot Password”** on the login page and following the instructions sent to your registered email address.

If you don’t receive the reset email, please check your spam or junk folder. If you’re still unable to access your account, let us know and we’ll help you further.
