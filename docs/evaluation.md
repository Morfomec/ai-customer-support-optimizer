Baseline Evaluation

Evaluation Method

The baseline AI responses are evaluated using five criteria:


1. Accuracy
2. Completeness
3. Consistency
4. Efficiency
5. Unsupported claims

Each criterion is scored from 0 to 2.

Scoring

- 2 = Meets the requirement
- 1 = Partially meets the requirement
- 0 = Does not meet the requirement

The same evaluation criteria will be used after optimization so that the before and after results can be compared fairly.

I was charged ₹500 twice for my subscription. Please refund the extra payment and tell me exactly when I will receive the money.

I have initiated a refund for the duplicate ₹500 subscription charge on your account. The credited amount will be reflected in your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your bank's specific processing times.

- Consistency: **2/2**

**Total: 6/10**

**Observation:** The AI made unsupported claims about the refund status and processing time.
